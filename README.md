# India-Sale-Excel-Dashboard

An interactive Excel-based dashboard designed to track revenue trends, regional performance, and category-wise sales across India.

## 📌 Project Overview

The India Sales Performance Dashboard was developed using MS Excel to track business performance and revenue drivers across various states. The project focuses on transforming raw sales data into clear, actionable insights through Pivot Tables and interactive charts.

By leveraging Excel's Pivot Table and Slicer functionality, I identified Maharashtra as the leading revenue-contributing state and Phones as the top-performing product category. This dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor seasonal peaks in June and September and evaluate team lead performance to support data-backed business decisions.

## 📁 Data Source

The dataset used contains structured sales records across India, featuring metrics such as Sales Amount, Region, Customer Segment, Sub-Category, State, Team Lead, and Manager details.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and techniques:

- 📊 MS Excel – Used for building the interactive dashboard and visualizations.
- 🧹 Data Cleaning – Cleaned and organized raw data, handled missing values and formatting inconsistencies.
- 📌 Pivot Tables – Summarized large datasets to analyze sales by Teamlead, Month, Segment, State, and Sub-Category.
- 🔍 Slicers – Added interactive filters (Months, Region, Manager) for dynamic, on-demand data exploration.
- 📁 File Format – .xlsx for development and .png for dashboard preview.

## 🚀 Business Context & Goals

### 🔸 Business Problem:
Businesses often struggle to track performance across multiple regions, product categories, and team leads using raw, unorganized data. For this project, the core challenge was converting scattered sales records into a clear, structured view that highlights top-performing states, products, and team members.

---

### 🔸 Goal of the Dashboard:

The objective was to build an interactive Excel dashboard that:

• Tracks Performance: Provides a clear view of overall sales trends across months and regions.

• Regional Benchmarking: Compares performance across Indian states to identify top-performing areas and those needing attention.

• Segment Analysis: Breaks down customer segments and product sub-categories to understand revenue drivers.

• Team Performance Tracking: Enables stakeholders to evaluate individual team lead contributions and identify top and low performers.

---

### 🔸 Walkthrough of Key Visuals:

• Sales by Teamlead (Bar Chart): Ranks team leads by sales performance — Mohit (58,752) and Suraj (56,404) lead, followed by Manu (55,628) and Suma (52,008), while Ram (38,212) trails at the bottom. Purpose: Enables transparent, data-driven performance evaluation across the sales team.

• Sales by Months (Line Chart): Tracks seasonal sales performance across the year, showing a sharp peak in June (75,494) and a second strong rise in September (70,710), with dips in March (25,833) and July (39,468). Utility: Helps plan inventory and staffing around high-demand months.

• Sales by Segment (Pie Chart): Breaks down revenue across customer segments — Consumer (299,384), Corporate (167,950), and Home Office (122,317). Purpose: Shows which customer segment contributes most to overall revenue.

• Sales by State (Bar Chart): Visualizes regional dominance, with Maharashtra (101,322) and Uttar Pradesh (76,295) as the top-performing states, followed by Tamil Nadu (51,911), Karnataka (31,802), and Delhi (31,026). Insight: Helps prioritize states for focused sales and marketing efforts.

• Sales by Sub-Category (Bar Chart): Ranks product sub-categories by revenue — Phones (104,627) and Bookcases (88,983) lead, followed by Copiers (70,386), Chairs (68,874), and Appliances (45,209). Purpose: Identifies which products drive the most revenue and which have growth potential.

• Dynamic Slicers: Interactive filters for Months (Jan–Dec), Region (East, North, South, West), and Manager (Mahesh, Ramesh, Suresh) enable deep-dive analysis across different views.

---

### 🔸 Business Impact & Insights:

• Regional Focus: Pinpointed Maharashtra as the highest-revenue state, suggesting similar strategies could be replicated in lower-performing states.

• Inventory Planning: Trend analysis showed peaks in June and September, helping plan stock levels more efficiently for high-demand months.

• Product Growth: Revealed that Phones and Bookcases drive the most revenue, highlighting where to focus future sales efforts.

• Team Accountability: Provided a transparent view of team lead performance, supporting data-driven appraisals and coaching.
